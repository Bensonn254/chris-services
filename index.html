Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book? +
For best availability, we recommend booking 4-6 weeks in advance. However, we can often accommodate last-minute bookings depending on our schedule. Contact us to check availability.
Do you accommodate dietary restrictions? +
Absolutely! We cater to all dietary needs including vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, halal, and any specific allergies. Just let us know your requirements when booking.
What's included in your catering service? +
Our full-service catering includes menu planning, food preparation, setup, serving staff (for larger events), and cleanup. We also provide serving equipment and table settings as needed.
What areas do you serve? +
We primarily serve Nairobi and surrounding counties. For events outside this area, please contact us to discuss travel arrangements.
What's your cancellation policy? +
Cancellations made 14+ days before the event receive a full refund minus booking fee. Cancellations within 7-14 days receive 50% refund. Less than 7 days notice is non-refundable.
